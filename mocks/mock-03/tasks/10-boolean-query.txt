Task 10 - Boolean Query

Scenario
A user is looking for available war-themed action movies but does not want removed titles or content from certain studios.

Task
Run a search against the streaming_catalog index.

Requirements
The query must:
- Search for the term war in title and synopsis.
- Require genre to be equal to action.
- Require available to be equal to true.
- Require duration_minutes to be greater than or equal to 90.
- Exclude documents where removed is equal to true.
- Exclude content from the following studios: ShadowPeak, BlueWave Docs.
- Give greater relevance to content directed by Christopher Wren (boost 4).
- Give greater relevance to titles with maturity_rating equal to R (boost 2).
- Return the first 10 results.
- Sort first by relevance and then by imdb_rating in descending order.
