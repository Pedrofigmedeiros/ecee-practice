Task 10 - Boolean Query

Scenario
A user is looking for available professional tools but does not want discontinued products or products from certain brands.

Task
Run a search against the products index.

Requirements
The query must:
- Search for the term drill in title and description.
- Require category to be equal to tools.
- Require available to be equal to true.
- Require stock to be greater than or equal to 5.
- Exclude documents where discontinued is equal to true.
- Exclude products from the following brands: Worker, Black+Decker.
- Give greater relevance to products from the following brands: Bosch, Makita.
- Return the first 10 results.
- Sort first by relevance and then by rating in descending order.
